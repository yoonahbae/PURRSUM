How it works

Three seconds, start to paste.

  1. 01

    Click your cat

    It sits in the corner of your screen. Click it and every monitor dims, ready for you to pick numbers.

  2. 02

    Box the numbers, anywhere

    Drag over numbers in any app. Hold Shift to box more, on any screen. Let go or press Enter to add up.

  3. 03

    Paste the total

    It's already copied, without commas. The bubble lists every number, numbered 1, 2, 3, so you can check them against the document.

Why PurrSum

If you can see it, you can add it up.

  • Any format, any screen

    It reads what's on your screen, not the file. So it works in PDFs, Excel, Google Sheets, Outlook, websites and scanned pages, on every monitor at once. Need more? Add from another window or type a number in.

  • Reads numbers the way people write them

    1641. 60 stays one number. $ ₩ € £ ¥ and commas are fine. Dates, invoice codes like INV-24081 and sizes like 12*1800 are skipped, so only real amounts get added.

  • Total copied, ready to paste

    The total lands on your clipboard without commas, ready for a cell or a form. Click any number in the bubble to leave it out, and the total updates.

  • Private by design

    Numbers are read right on your computer. Nothing you select is uploaded, so client invoices and statements stay yours.

Questions

Does it work with Excel and Google Sheets?

Yes. It reads the numbers you can see on screen, so it works in Excel, Google Sheets, PDFs, emails, websites, even screenshots and scans. You don't need to open or upload the file.

Can I add numbers from different windows or monitors?

Yes. Hold Shift to draw boxes on any screen in one go, or click "Add more from screen" in the bubble to keep adding from another window.

Is it safe? Why the warning?

The warning only means the app isn't signed with a paid Microsoft certificate yet, which is normal for new, free apps. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and never sends anything off your computer.

Does it upload my documents anywhere?

No. Numbers are read on your own computer. It only goes online once during setup, to download its number-reading part.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. It's Windows 10 and 11 for now; a Mac version is coming.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find PurrSum Noir, and choose Uninstall.
