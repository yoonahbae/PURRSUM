Download for Windows
  1. 1

    Step 1: Open the file

    Open Install PurrSum Noir.exe from your Downloads folder. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Keep.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Blue "Windows protected your PC" screen

    This is normal for new apps from small makers. Click the small More info link under the message. A Run anyway button appears: click it.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Security pop-up? Click Allow Once

    Work computers with security software (like Trend Micro) may show "Newly Encountered Program Detected". Click Allow Once.

    Be quick: it blocks the installer on its own after about 30 seconds. If that happens, just open the file again.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Click Install, then pick your cat

    Stay online for about a minute while it sets up. Then pick a buddy, give it a name, and it moves into the corner of your screen.

    It's also on your Desktop and in the Start menu. To pin it: open Start, right-click PurrSum Noir, choose Pin to taskbar.

Questions

Does it work with Excel and Google Sheets?

Yes. It reads the numbers you can see on screen, so it works in Excel, Google Sheets, PDFs, emails, websites, even screenshots and scans. You don't need to open or upload the file.

Can I add numbers from different windows or monitors?

Yes. Click "+ Add more from screen" in the bubble to keep adding from another window or monitor. If you like shortcuts, just tap Shift while the bubble is open: it does the same as Add more. Holding Shift while you drag also lets you draw several boxes in one go.

Is it safe? Why the warnings?

The warnings only mean the app isn't signed with a paid Microsoft certificate yet, which is normal for new, free apps. Work antivirus (like the "Newly Encountered Program" pop-up) flags any program it hasn't seen before, so Allow Once is fine for this file. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and never sends anything off your computer.

Does it upload my documents anywhere?

No. Numbers are read on your own computer. It only goes online once during setup, to download its number-reading part.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. It's Windows 10 and 11 for now; a Mac version is coming.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find PurrSum Noir, and choose Uninstall.
