Why PurrSum

Five things it does better than a calculator and a squint.

  • Any app, any screen

    PDFs in Adobe Acrobat, Outlook emails, websites, scanned statements. Drag the cat to your other monitor and it works there too.

  • Reads invoices like you do

    1641. 60 stays one number. (75.25) and 300.00- count as negatives. Dates, times, INV-24081, PO#55 and sizes like 12*1800 are skipped.

  • Hold Shift to skip rows

    Draw as many boxes as you need, even across monitors. Each one gets a numbered badge, so you always know where a number came from.

  • Private by design

    The numbers are read right on your computer. Nothing you select is ever uploaded, so client invoices and bank statements stay yours.

  • Total copied, ready to paste

    The total lands on your clipboard without commas, ready for Excel or your entry form. Click any number in the bubble to leave it out and the total updates.

Three seconds, start to paste.

  1. 01

    Click your cat

    Every screen dims, ready for you to pick the numbers.

  2. 02

    Drag over the numbers

    Hold Shift for more boxes. Let go, or press Enter, to add them up. Esc cancels.

  3. 03

    Paste the total

    It's already copied. The bubble shows every number found, so you can double-check at a glance.
